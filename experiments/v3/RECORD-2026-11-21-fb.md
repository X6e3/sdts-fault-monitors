## Diagnosis after the verdict (not deciding; 2026-11-21, from the run files, nothing rerun)

**The b run that lost 45 samples** (fb-450022 drift_0.0005 E1 ramp):
- Its event and its E0 twin's have the smallest certified margins of all 900 events: in none, F3 detects both at
  t\* = 764 with margin −0.0223 mC.
- In b, the margin at 764 is +0.0817 mC, so no alarm is raised there. 139 fold directions failed on this run and
  were replaced by implied bounds; together they moved the margin at 764 by 0.104 mC. b's first alarm is at 809
  (−17.07 mC, certified), which is also v2's detection.
- Its E0 twin has the same event (margin −0.0223 mC at 764). In b, 117 of its fold directions failed, on other folds
  (the injections are a hash of the row key), and its margin at 764 moved by 3.4e-5 mC only: detected at 764.
- DEV's smallest margins are similar: −0.0235 mC (430009 drift_0.001, both arms, at 1452) and −0.0385 mC (430026
  drift_0.0005). Their b runs were not delayed.
- **INFERRED:** an implied bound costs a fraction of a millikelvin. That matters only on an event this close to the
  edge, and only if failures fall on the folds that decide it. It is a cost in timing, not in soundness: the alarm
  at 809 is certified.
- Not examined: whether the 0.104 mC comes from one direction or several. That would need a replay with each
  fold's bounds logged.
